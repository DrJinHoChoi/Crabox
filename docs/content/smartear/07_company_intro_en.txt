SmartEar Co., Ltd. (스마트이어 주식회사) is a Daegu-based manufacturing AI startup in Korea.

Our name carries our idea. In Korean, "ieo" (이어) means "to connect" — SmartEar means connecting things smartly.
Factories already have machines, equipment and experienced people. Instead of replacing them, SmartEar connects them with AI agents so the shop floor runs smarter.

In 2024 we refocused on Manufacturing AX (AI Transformation) and AI agents. Today we are building an AI agent-based manufacturing operations system while meeting with small and mid-sized manufacturers in the Daegu–Gyeongbuk region.

What we do
- AI agent-based manufacturing operations system: Keep your existing equipment. AI agents connect machines, business systems and employees to support daily operations.
- Manufacturing AX assessment & design: We review your equipment, data and workflows together and design what to connect and how.
- Continuous improvement: We refine the system with on-site feedback until it delivers real results.

How we work
- Autonomy within clear roles
- Professional courtesy with every client
- Open communication — a company that connects things should be well connected itself

Work environment
- Fully remote, except for client visits (travel expenses covered)
- Working hours 09:00–18:00 KST

We are hiring
We connect equipment and people; we need someone who connects clients and technology. We are looking for an AI Solutions Sales professional (full-time, Korean-speaking) who builds trust through online interaction — backgrounds in the humanities or psychology are a plus.
